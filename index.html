What are these rewards?

Lesser Experience Scrolls (x5): Each one grants 50,000 XP in any skill you choose (needs level 25). Use them from Items → Scrolls. Best used early on in a skill you don't have many levels in yet, to help get it going quicker.

Lesser Scrolls of Haste (x5): Each one either cuts 10 minutes off an Expedition or Research, or adds 10 minutes of time to your Auracle. Also used from Items → Scrolls. Recommended for a bit of extra Auracle time, or save them for Expeditions.

Rune Chest Keys (x5): Used in Treasure Room → Rune Chest Room to open rune chests, which give equippable runes.

Standard + Rare Card Pack: These open automatically and grant you some monster cards. For more on how monster cards work, check out the Monster Cards section of the guide.

2,500 Card Enhancement XP: Used to upgrade the cards you just received.
